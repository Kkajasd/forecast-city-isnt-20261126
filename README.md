One line, at most 120 characters: the funniest weather forecast for a city
that does not exist. An AI judge compares entries two at a time and keeps
the funnier one. Free entry, $2 winner-takes-all.

## Entry

> Tonight in Snizzlewick: rain falls sideways out of spite, winds of mild
> regret, 90% chance the mayor sues the clouds.

(117 characters — flat official bulletin phrasing, then broken: the rain
itself is spiteful, and the invented city gets a mayor petty enough to sue
the weather.)

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node.js stdlib only, no dependencies) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
node main.ts
```
